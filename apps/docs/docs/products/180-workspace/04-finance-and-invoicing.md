# Finance & Invoicing

The **180 Finance & Invoicing** module streamlines commercial receivables, recurring client subscriptions, and accounting reconciliation. Seamlessly bridged to **180 Pay**, invoices issued from 180 Workspace feature one-click checkout links allowing clients to settle balances in their local currency via UPI, credit card, bank transfer, or crypto.

```mermaid
graph LR
    Draft[Draft Invoice] --> Review[Manager Review & Send]
    Review --> Client[Client Receives 1-Click Link]
    Client --> PayEngine[180 Pay Gateway Rails]
    PayEngine --> Settled[Automated Ledger Reconciliation]
    Settled --> Receipt[Tax Receipt Dispatched]
```

---

## 1. Dynamic Multi-Currency Invoicing

Create compliant tax invoices for domestic and cross-border clients in seconds:

- **Multi-Currency Support**: Bill clients in USD, EUR, GBP, INR, CAD, AUD, and more with automated exchange rate conversion.
- **Tax Profiles & Regional Compliance**: Configure GST, VAT, and Sales Tax profiles with customizable tax registration numbers (GSTIN, VAT ID).
- **Line-Item Timesheet Ingestion**: Pull billable hours logged in **180 HR & Projects** directly into invoice line items with one click.
- **Custom Branding**: Apply your organization's logo, brand colors, custom payment terms, and legal disclaimers.

---

## 2. 1-Click Instant Payment Checkout

Every invoice generated by 180 Workspace provides a secure, hosted payment portal:

- **Zero-Friction Payment Options**: Clients can settle via UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, Netbanking, or international wire.
- **Instant Settlement Updates**: When a payment completes, the invoice status changes immediately from `PENDING` to `PAID` via the Sovereign Work Graph.
- **Automated Payment Receipts**: Generates downloadable PDF tax receipts and dispatches confirmation notifications automatically.

---

## 3. Recurring Retainers & Subscription Cycles

For agencies and SaaS businesses with recurring client agreements:
- **Automated Billing Schedules**: Generate and send invoices automatically on monthly, quarterly, or annual intervals.
- **Automated Payment Retries (Dunning)**: Failed transactions trigger automated, gentle reminders with alternate payment links.
- **Credit Notes & Adjustments**: Issue partial or full refunds and credit adjustments while preserving double-entry accounting integrity.

---

## 4. Ledger & Cash Flow Analytics

- **Accounts Receivable Aging**: Track overdue accounts categorized by aging buckets (0-30 days, 31-60 days, 60+ days).
- **Revenue Projections**: Real-time forecasts combining recurring retainer agreements and weighted pipeline deals.
- **Audit-Ready Export**: Export financial manifests in CSV, JSON, and PDF formats for straightforward accountant and auditor handoffs.
